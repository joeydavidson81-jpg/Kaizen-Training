# 🌍 Speech Translator - User Guide

## Welcome! 👋

This is a simple tool for translating speech between **English and Mandarin Chinese** in real-time.

---

## Getting Started (2 minutes)

### 1. Open the App
- Click the link you received: `https://your-domain-here.vercel.app`
- The app opens in your browser ✅

### 2. Allow Microphone Access
- Your browser will ask for permission to use your microphone
- Click **"Allow"** ✅
- Your speech is NOT recorded or stored anywhere

### 3. Pick Your Languages
- **Source Language:** The language you'll speak in
- **Target Language:** The language you want to hear the translation in
- Use the **⇄** button to swap languages

---

## How to Use

### Basic Translation (Simple)

```
1. Click "Start Listening" button
   ↓
2. Speak clearly into your microphone
   ↓
3. See your speech in the "Transcript" box
   ↓
4. See translation in the "Translation" box
   ↓
5. Click "Stop" when done
```

### Example

| You Say | You Hear |
|---------|----------|
| "Good morning!" | "早上好！" |
| "What is your name?" | "你叫什么名字？" |
| "我喜欢中文" | "I like Chinese" |

## Tips for Best Results

### 📱 Microphone Setup
- Use a **quiet room** (less background noise)
- Sit **15-20 cm** from your microphone
- Speak **clearly and naturally** (no need to shout)
- Avoid loud background music or traffic

### 🗣️ Pronunciation
- **English:** Speak standard English (American, British, Australian all work)
- **Mandarin:** Standard Mandarin (普通话) works best
  - Dialects may not be recognized well
  - Clear pronunciation helps

### 💬 What Works Best
| ✅ Good | ❌ Avoid |
|--------|----------|
| Short sentences | Mumbling |
| Natural speech | Typing |
| Pauses between sentences | Background noise |
| Clear enunciation | Speaking too fast |

- Stops recording

### ⇄ Swap Languages
- Switches source ↔ target
- Useful for going back and forth

## Common Issues & Solutions

### ❓ "Microphone not found"
**Solution:**
1. Check your device has a microphone
2. Go to browser Settings → Privacy → Microphone
3. Make sure this website is allowed
4. Refresh the page (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R)
5. Try again

### ❓ "Nothing is happening when I speak"
**Solution:**
1. Check if microphone light is on
2. Speak louder and clearer
3. Reduce background noise
4. Check your browser supports Web Speech:
   - Chrome ✅ (best)
   - Edge ✅ (good)
   - Safari ✅ (good)
   - Firefox ⚠️ (limited)

### ❓ "Translation looks wrong"
**Solution:**
1. Sometimes translations are awkward but understandable
2. Speak more slowly
3. Use simpler words
4. Break long sentences into short ones
5. Try again - accuracy improves with practice

### ❓ "Getting 'Listening...' but nothing happens"
**Solution:**
1. Wait a few seconds (processing takes time)
2. Speak again after the "Listening..." indicator appears
3. Make sure your speech is loud enough
4. Refresh page and try again

### ❓ "Browser not supported"
**Solution:**
- Use Chrome, Edge, or Safari
- Firefox has limited support
- Update your browser to latest version
- Try a different browser if still having issues

## Privacy & Security

### 🔒 Your Data is Safe
- ✅ No recording of your voice
- ✅ No storage of your speech
- ✅ No tracking of your usage
- ✅ No cookies (except basic technical ones)
- ✅ HTTPS encryption (secure connection)

### 🎤 Microphone Access
- Access is **ONLY** while app is open
- Access is **ONLY** when you click "Start Listening"

- Browser won't share access with other websites

### 📤 Data Sent to Internet
Only the text you speak (not voice):
1. Your transcript → Processing
2. Translation request → Translation Service
3. Translation result → Back to your browser
4. **Nothing is stored anywhere**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Is my speech recorded?
**A:** No! Your voice is only processed locally by your browser. The app only sends the recognized **text** (not voice) for translation.

### Q: Can I use this offline?
**A:** No, you need internet for translation. Speech recognition works offline, but translation requires connection to translation service.

### Q: Can multiple people use at once?
**A:** Yes! Each person gets their own browser session. Everyone can use simultaneously.

### Q: How accurate is the translation?
**A:** ~85-90% accurate for common phrases and sentences. Complex sentences or technical terms may need manual review.

### Q: Can I use this on mobile phone?
**A:** Yes! Works on iPhone (Safari) and Android (Chrome). Mobile microphone quality may be lower.

### Q: Does it support other languages?
**A:** Currently: English ↔ Mandarin. Other languages can be added easily.

### Q: Is there a limit on how much I can use?
**A:** No! Free unlimited use. No login required.

### Q: Can I download translations?
**A:** Currently: No. Copy-paste the text if needed. Export feature coming soon.
